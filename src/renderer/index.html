          Streamer mode
          
          
        
          
        
          For streaming or recording your screen. Hides the QR code and link, folder paths (they contain your Windows user name), phone and file names, text and links from phones, the log and the PIN, including in notifications and the tray menu. Click a hidden item to show it for 10 seconds.
          
        
        
          
        
          Also worth doing: give this PC a name that isn't your real name (iPhones nearby see it), and turn on Do Not Disturb on a phone you mirror on stream.
